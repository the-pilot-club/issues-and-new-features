# Welcome to the TPC Ideas and Roadmaps Repository!

Welcome to The Pilot Club (TPC) Ideas and Roadmaps repository! This is a central hub where our members can contribute ideas and track the progress of our projects. Whether you have an idea for a new feature, a Discord bot, an enhancement to our TPC Website, or improvements for our OnAir Virtual Airline (VA), this is the place to share and collaborate.

---

## Purpose of This Repository

The Ideas and Roadmaps repository serves three main purposes:

1. **Idea Collection**: Provide a platform for all TPC members to suggest new features, tools, or improvements.
2. **Project Tracking**: Keep everyone updated on the progress and status of various projects.
3. **Collaboration**: Foster a space where members can discuss and refine ideas before they are implemented.

---

## How to Use This Repository

### Submitting Your Ideas
To share your ideas, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Category**:
   - **Discord Bots**: Suggestions for new bots, features for existing bots, or other bot-related functionality.
   - **TPC Website**: Ideas to enhance the user experience, introduce new tools, or improve existing features.
   - **OnAir VA**: Features, tools, or improvements for The Pilot Club's Virtual Airline on OnAir.

2. **Create a New Issue**:
   - Click on the "Issues" tab at the top of this repository.
   - Select "New Issue."
   - Use the appropriate template for your category (e.g., "Discord Bot Idea," "Website Enhancement," "OnAir VA Improvement").

3. **Provide Details**:
   - Give your issue a clear and descriptive title.
   - In the description, explain your idea in detail. Be sure to include:
     - The problem your idea solves or the value it adds.
     - Any specific features or functionalities.
     - Relevant examples or references, if applicable.

4. **Label Your Issue**:
   - Use the labels provided to categorize your idea (e.g., "Discord Bot," "Website," "OnAir VA").

### Engaging With Other Ideas

- Browse existing issues to see what others have suggested.
- Comment on ideas to provide feedback, suggestions, or support.
- Use the "Thumbs Up" reaction to show your support for an idea.

---

## How Ideas Are Processed

1. **Initial Review**: Submitted ideas will be reviewed by the TPC development team.
2. **Discussion**: If more clarity or input is needed, we will ask questions or open the floor for discussion.
3. **Prioritization**: Ideas will be evaluated based on feasibility, impact, and alignment with TPC’s goals.
4. **Implementation**: Approved ideas will be added to the roadmap and assigned to the appropriate team members.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

- Be respectful and constructive in your suggestions and feedback.
- Ensure your ideas align with TPC's mission and values.
- Avoid submitting duplicate ideas; search the Issues tab before creating a new one.
